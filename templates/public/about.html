Optional Google connection

A familiar sign-in, and dates where you need them

You can use Abby with your family username and password. Connecting Google is optional and links a Google account to an existing Abby account; it does not create a new family or account.

  1. Sign in with your Abby username and password.
  2. Open Settings → Google account → Connect Google account and review Google's consent screen.
  3. After linking, you can use Sign in with Google for that Abby account.

If you also turn on Enable calendar sync in Settings, Abby can create and update Google Calendar events for project deadlines, chore schedules, and completed work sessions. Sync is off by default.

Parents can also choose individual Google Drive files for project resources or import PDFs into a plan they review before creating a venture. Drive selection asks for a separate selected-file permission. It does not require Calendar sync or change the file's Google sharing settings. Read about selected files and PDF processing.

The current Google connection requests Calendar event permission during linking and sign-in, even when sync is off. It uses your Google identity and email for the account link and stores encrypted authorization credentials to support sign-in and background sync. Read how Google data is used and how to disconnect.
