Your family's information

Privacy policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

The Abby Project is a family app for projects, chores, homework, work sessions, skills, rewards, and personal journals. This policy explains how the app uses information entered by family members and information connected through Google.

Family information and access

Abby stores account and family details and the content you choose to add, including projects, chores, homework, time records, journal entries, and attachments. It uses these records to provide family planning, progress, rewards, and memory features.

Parents manage accounts and family responsibilities. Account access is scoped to the family and the member's role. Personal journal and academic reflection entries are private by default. Their author chooses whether to share them with parents; being a parent does not grant ordinary in-app access to another member's private journal.

Deployment operators administer the servers, database, backups, and support systems. Private in-app content is not encrypted against those operators; administrative access can include stored account records and content. Contact the operator with privacy or access questions.

App usage measurements

Abby records a signed-in member's foreground visit at most once per UTC day and selected successful actions, such as submitting work, logging reading or movement, checking a habit, requesting a reward and approving work. Usage records contain account and family links, the member's role, a timestamp, an action category and an internal saved-record reference used to avoid counting retries twice.

These measurements do not collect journal content, written notes, project or assignment titles, files, navigation URLs, IP addresses or screen recordings. Automated background work and assistant calls are excluded from human action counts. Designated live-check households are excluded from usage reports.

Staff parents can read aggregate usage reports for their own family. A deployment superuser can also read aggregate totals across families. Reports do not list member names or the contents of saved work. Daily cleanup removes individual usage events older than 90 days; deleting an account or closing its family also removes its linked usage events. Operational logs and backups have the separate retention limits described below.

AI features and external assistants

Abby uses language models for project ideas, import suggestions, and optional homework and project planning. These services receive the content described below. Avoid including information you do not want the selected provider to process. Generated suggestions can be inaccurate; review them before relying on them.

  • Project ideas: when the Projects page requests ideas, a deployment-configured Anthropic service can receive completed project titles, categories and difficulty, and skill names and levels. Abby offers predefined ideas when this service is unavailable.
  • URL and PDF import: when deployment AI import is enabled, Anthropic receives the source title and up to 12,000 characters of extracted text to suggest categories, steps, materials, resources and a summary. This includes text from a PDF you select from Google Drive. The source form names Anthropic (Claude) and this transfer before you choose Parse Source or Import PDF from Google Drive, for URL, local PDF and Drive imports. Processing happens during import, before you review which suggestions to keep. Import suggestions are optional to apply; that later choice does not undo processing already performed.
  • Homework planning: choosing to plan sends the assignment title, subject, effort level, due date and description to the family's configured OpenAI or Anthropic service. The result is saved as a linked homework plan. Parents can plan; children can plan their own eligible assignments when the advance-planning rules allow it.
  • Project planning: a parent can request a draft using the family's configured OpenAI or Anthropic service. It receives the project brief and guidance and, when extending a project, bounded existing milestones, steps, completion indicators and materials. The parent reviews the proposed items before saving them.

Parents manage their family's provider, model and encrypted API key in Settings → Planning AI. Removing that connection stops new family planning requests; it does not delete existing plans or disable the deployment's project ideas and import enrichment. The deployment operator manages those separate services. OpenAI planning requests disable stored response state, but this does not promise that the provider keeps no records. Provider processing, human access and retention depend on the actual provider account and terms; ask the operator or the parent managing the connection for details.

External assistants through MCP: an authorized assistant can call Abby's tools to read or change data allowed by its permissions and the connected account's current role. Tool results are sent to that external client and can include family project and homework content, progress, resource links, photographs and journal or reflection entries the account is permitted to read. Private-entry and family restrictions continue to apply. The assistant's provider, conversation storage and deletion controls are separate from Abby's planning connection. Authorize a client only if you intend it to process the accessible information and perform the actions you request.

Imported content saved into a project, including Google-derived text, can later be included in project ideas or planning context, or returned to an authorized external assistant. Revoking a connection cannot retrieve information already sent to a provider or client. Abby does not operate a model-training or fine-tuning feature and does not retain information obtained through Google Workspace APIs to develop, improve or train non-personalized AI or machine-learning models. The operator must also ensure that external recipients' use meets the Google data restrictions described below.

Voice dictation: starting the microphone in the journal editor uses your browser's speech recognition service to turn audio into draft text. Depending on the browser, audio may be processed by an external speech service; Abby does not require processing to stay on the device. Abby saves the submitted text, and this dictation feature does not upload audio recordings to Abby. Check your browser's permission and speech-service controls.

Abby no longer provides AI sprite or image generation or rerolling. The app continues to display supplied artwork and existing assets. Homework proof photos are not automatically judged by a language model, and rewards and account permissions are controlled by Abby's application rules.

Google account connection

Google connection is optional. You can use Abby's core family features with an Abby username and password. Google sign-in works only after a Google account is linked to an existing Abby account through Settings.

During authorization, Abby requests openid, email and basic profile permission, and https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.events.owned for events on calendars you own. The current flow requests Calendar event permission during both linking and later Google sign-in, including before calendar sync is enabled.

Abby verifies Google's identity token and stores your Google account identifier and email address to recognize the linked account. It stores access tokens, refresh tokens when provided, token expiry and scope information in encrypted credentials. This lets the server maintain the connection without repeatedly asking you to sign in. It also stores mappings between Abby records and the Google Calendar events it creates.

Basic profile permission is part of the authorization request; the current integration does not import your Google profile photo or contact list. It does not access Gmail. Optional Drive file selection and uploads use the separate permission described below.

Optional Google Calendar sync

Calendar sync is off by default. Abby uses the Calendar connection only when you enable Enable calendar sync in Settings. It creates and updates events for project deadlines, chore schedules, and completed work sessions. Events can include titles, dates, times, reminders, and chore reward details from Abby.

Events go to the linked account's primary Google Calendar by default. A different calendar you own can be configured through the app's calendar settings API. Abby keeps the identifiers of its created events so it can update or remove the corresponding event when app behavior calls for it. The current integration does not import unrelated Calendar events into Abby or use them to build a personal profile.

Information sent to Google Calendar is handled by Google under its own policies and the calendar's sharing settings. People who can view that calendar may be able to see synced event information.

Optional Google Drive files and uploads

Parents can choose individual Drive files as project resources, upload files through the resource picker, or import a school PDF or project guide. The picker requests https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file, which grants access to files you select for Abby and permits creating files you upload through the picker. It does not request access to your entire Drive. The picker can use a Google account without linking it for Abby sign-in or enabling Calendar sync.

The short-lived Drive access token stays in memory for the current operation and is sent over HTTPS to Abby to verify file details, retrieve a selected PDF, or save a file already stored in Abby to Drive. Abby does not save it in its database, browser storage, import jobs, or background-task messages. It does not use your stored Calendar credentials for Drive selection or uploads.

In Add Resource, the picker's Upload tab sends PDFs, photos, and other files from your device directly to Google Drive, with a choice of destination folder. Google stores the uploaded file under the account's Drive and folder permissions; anyone with access under those permissions may be able to view it. Resource uploads do not send the file contents to Abby or the AI import provider. Abby saves the resource's title, type, and link only after you choose Add Resource.

A file already uploaded may remain in Google Drive if you cancel the picker or resource form, change Abby accounts, or Abby cannot verify or save its link. Remove unwanted files directly in Google Drive.

Save to Google Drive lets parents copy an imported PDF or an accessible project, approved homework proof, or creation photo already stored in Abby directly to My Drive in the Google account they confirm. Only the importing parent can export an imported PDF; photos follow their existing family and project permissions. The original stays in Abby. This export sends the chosen file to Google and does not send it to an AI provider or change its sharing permissions. Each save makes a new copy. If a network error or account change makes the result uncertain, check My Drive before retrying because a copy may already exist.

A project resource stores the file title, type, and Google link. Opening that link still requires the viewer's Google Drive access; Abby does not change the file's sharing permissions. PDF import saves a copy of the selected file and a reviewable plan. If AI import is enabled for this deployment, the source title and bounded extracted PDF text are sent to Anthropic to suggest the plan, as explained in AI features and external assistants. The import screen discloses this before selection. Only the plan you review and confirm becomes a project.

PDF import is a separate action that selects an existing PDF; its picker has no Upload tab. Disconnecting Google does not remove files uploaded to Drive, saved resource links, copied PDFs, or import plans. Manage uploaded files in Google Drive. Ask the deployment operator to remove retained PDF copies, including discarded imports and backup copies, under this deployment's retention practices.

How Google data is used and protected

Abby uses Google data for linked-account sign-in, optional Calendar sync, and selected Drive resources, uploads, and PDFs described here. Saved sign-in and Calendar authorization credentials are encrypted at rest, and the production connection uses HTTPS. Encryption does not replace the deployment operator's responsibility to protect server access and backups.

Abby does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or transfer it to advertising platforms or data brokers. Sign-in and Calendar credentials are not included in AI prompts. Selected PDF content may be processed by Anthropic for import suggestions, and saved content can reach later planning or authorized external assistants as described above.

Hosting and backup systems process stored data as part of operating this deployment. Operators must limit human access to Google user data to the user's affirmative permission for specific data, necessary security investigation, or legal obligations. Operational access and any transfer of Google user data must follow the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Your controls and disconnection

  • Turn off sync: disable Enable calendar sync in Settings to stop new Calendar sync operations.
  • Unlink in Abby: choose unlink Google account in Settings. Abby deletes its stored Google account credentials and event mappings. Google sign-in for that Abby account stops working until you link it again.
  • Revoke at Google: remove Abby's access through your Google Account's third-party connections.
  • Remove Calendar events: delete existing Abby events directly in Google Calendar if you no longer want them.

Unlinking in Abby does not revoke Google's authorization grant or delete previously created Calendar events. Turning off sync also leaves existing events in place. Revoking Google permission prevents authorized API access; you can additionally unlink in Abby to remove its stored connection.

Older connections may retain a previously granted, broader Calendar permission. To remove that earlier grant and use the narrower permission now requested by Abby, revoke Abby's access at Google, then unlink and reconnect in Abby.

Account deletion and retention

Settings includes password-confirmed self-deletion. A departing parent must choose another active parent to keep shared authored work; a child can delete only their own account. Private journals are deleted with their owner and never transferred. If you do not have an Abby password, ask a family parent or the deployment operator for a password reset first. Deactivation blocks sign-in but is not deletion.

The last active parent can separately close the whole family after reviewing the impact and confirming the family identifier. Closure deletes every child and inactive parent account, private journals, shared work and family settings, including saved AI/provider and printer credentials. Other families and global catalogs remain. Transferring the family contact does not grant extra permissions: parents keep equal access.

Deleting a member removes their account and related personal records from the active database. Projects and homework assigned to them and personal habits can also disappear. During self-deletion, shared authored work passes to the departing parent's selected active successor; a child's shared homework, chores and habits automatically pass to an active parent in their family. If no active parent is available, that shared work must be resolved before self-deletion. Other members' saved progress and rewards remain. The separate parent-managed member deletion can still remove projects and homework that member created, including work involving remaining members. Self-deletion and family closure remove or redact structurally linked activity; historical unattributed summaries, notifications and other surviving records may still contain the member's name or shared text. This is not comprehensive anonymization. Family-owned settings and credentials remain after an individual departure.

Files attached to deleted records, including avatars, photos, audio, imported PDFs, receipts and print files, are scheduled for storage cleanup. Failed cleanup is recorded and retried; database deletion does not mean every file has already been erased. A file still referenced by a surviving record is retained. This process does not recover files already orphaned before cleanup tracking was introduced.

Deleting an account removes its linked Google credentials and Calendar mappings from the application's active database. It does not revoke the Google grant, remove existing Calendar events, or delete copies exported to Drive. Downloaded copies and device drafts may also remain on the devices where they were saved.

Operational logs, storage object versions and copies in deployment backups can have different retention behavior and are not purged by this operation; no fixed deletion period for those copies is promised here. Ask the deployment operator about this deployment's retention and backup practices.

Questions and policy changes

For privacy questions, Google data deletion requests, or questions about the operator of this deployment, use the app's support email shown on Google's consent screen or ask the family parent who gave you access.

This page will be updated when the app's information practices change. New uses of Google data require appropriate disclosure and consent before those uses begin.
