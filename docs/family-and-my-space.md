# Family and My space

Parents can manage their household and take part in Abby themselves with the
same account. Use **Family / My space** at the top of the app to switch. Your
account keeps its parent permissions in both spaces; your progress belongs to
you, separately from your children's progress.

## Family

Family has four main destinations:

- **Today:** your family overview, work awaiting review, and reward deliveries.
- **Tasks:** assign and plan homework, chores, habits, projects, and challenges.
  Choose a child to work with their records.
- **Children:** each child's saved level, Coins, skills, badges, and pets, with
  links to their tasks and shared memories. This overview is read-only.
- **Rewards:** manage the family shop, requests, delivery, timecards, payments,
  and 3D print requests.

Household setup, co-parents, activity history, and account preferences remain
available in the profile menu. An unavailable review source is shown as
unavailable, rather than an empty queue.
Family's project list can include projects assigned to parents as well as
children, including projects started in My space. Family keeps its payment and
assignment controls: deliberately adding a bonus there follows the usual
Family payment rules. Use My space to complete your own work for personal
progression without cash pay.

## My space

My space gives a parent the personal experience: **Today, Tasks, Pets, Rewards,
Progress, and Memories**. Children keep their existing personal navigation.

Track your own habits, reading, movement, chores, projects, creations, and
journal. Your saved activities can earn your own XP, Coins, drops, badges,
daily bonuses, and automatic or solo challenge rewards. Hatch and care for
your own pets, use your inventory, and spend your own Coins in the shop.
Existing reward approval and delivery steps still apply.
Today's daily bonus and saved challenge progress appear in My space. Opening
a challenge's details records that opening for your own challenge history.
Checking Family does not clear the personal catch-up summary on your next
visit to My space.
Your birthday, streak, and Perfect Day celebrations wait for My space, so a
Family visit does not use them up.

Your own chores are confirmed when you mark them done. Children still submit
chores for parent review. New personal chores and projects do not promise
allowance or cash payments in My space; personal progression uses XP and Coins.
Imported projects belong to you in My space. Personal project and milestone
completion in My space earns progression without adding cash payments; previously recorded
payments stay in the payment history.
Personal chores remain in Tasks for editing or deletion when paused, during an
off week, or after a one-time completion. You can reactivate a paused chore;
completion still follows its schedule and saved completion history.
If you later assign a personal project
to a child in Family, its hourly rate starts with that child's usual rate unless
you choose a specific project rate.
An unpaid rate you explicitly choose in Family stays unpaid when you reassign
the project, including after editing or completing it in My space.
School homework and grades remain in the child experience and Family tools.
Family cooperative challenge assignments continue to select children.

The savings tracker retains its existing money-owed meaning. It does not
represent a bank balance or turn your Coins into cash.

## Switching and privacy

Each space remembers its last page for your account in the current browser
tab. Finish or close an open form before switching; unfinished work and busy
saves keep the other space unavailable. Switching clears the previous view's
forms and data before loading the new space. Your Family review indicator
remains visible in My space.
Opening a notification also remembers its destination space in this tab, so
reloading or continuing to another page keeps you in that space.

My space uses your own records. Selecting a child in Family never signs in as
that child. Children cannot switch into Family or gain parent permissions.

Parent journals are private by default. Sharing a parent journal means sharing
with co-parents in the same family; it does not give children access. Your
personal bookshelf and reading notes stay with your own account. A child's
private journal remains private, including in the Children overview.
Your manually added personal memories, personal challenge progress, and new
personal challenge completion activity are private to you. Shared challenge
catalogs stay available to the family. Earlier household activity remains in
the family history.
Notifications about a child's pet open that child's Family overview. Pet
receipts that identify you as their owner open your Pets. Household copies and
older replies that do not identify an owner open Family.
Replies to a child's shared journal or grades open that child's Family view.
Replies to your shared journal, your savings receipts, and your own chore or
project completion receipts open My space. A child's savings notification opens
their Family overview.
