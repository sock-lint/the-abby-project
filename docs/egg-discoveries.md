# Egg discoveries

Egg discoveries let you choose one egg to work toward through reading or
listening, movement, and making or practice. When discoveries are available,
they replace new system egg challenges, random eggs, shop eggs and expedition
egg rolls. Food, potions, Coins and other rewards keep their normal sources.
If discoveries are unavailable, the earlier earning rules remain in place.

## Choose an egg

Open **Tasks → Challenges** and find **Egg discoveries**. Choose an available
egg to see its requirements. Only one discovery is active at a time. Choosing
another pauses the previous one and saves its progress. Resume a paused
discovery whenever you want to continue it.

An egg may be unavailable because you already earned its discovery or companion, it is
incubating, its seasonal start window is closed, or you have not yet unlocked
rare discoveries. The page explains the reason. Rare discoveries require
hatching two different companion species; two appearances of one species
count as one.

Creature appearances remain a surprise until hatching. Choosing an egg or
viewing its requirements does not hatch it or grant ownership.

## Make progress

New reading and movement sessions logged for your family's current day advance
the active discovery. Their normal rewards keep their existing rules. Reading
minutes can advance an egg after today's Reading Adventure has already been
earned; movement can advance an egg after its daily XP allowance is used.

Use **Log making or practice** for activities such as drawing, building or
music practice. Enter the activity name and minutes. You do not need to create
a project or ask a parent to assign its reward. This activity log contributes
egg progress; it does not add another Coin or XP reward.

Short sessions add together. Longer sessions contribute more minutes. There
are no consecutive-day requirements. Splitting a session into several logs
does not produce extra progress or searches.

Log each activity once. Retrying an uncertain save uses its original request;
deleting or editing a saved session cannot rearm its egg credit. Past-day logs,
old activity history and sessions saved without an active discovery do not
move into a later target. Minutes already assigned to one egg cannot be reused
for another.

## Ordinary discoveries

An ordinary egg needs **240 minutes total**, including at least **90 minutes
in each of two activity types**. The remaining 60 minutes may come from any
type. For example, 150 minutes of making and 90 minutes of reading qualify.
240 minutes of reading alone do not meet the variety requirement.

## Rare discoveries

Each search uses **60 minutes of balanced activity**, with at most 30 supplied
by one type. You can mix two or three types. For example, 30 reading minutes
and 30 movement minutes qualify, as do 30 reading, 15 movement and 15 making.
Extra minutes from one type remain with that egg until enough other activity
balances them. All partial minutes stay recorded.

Searches happen automatically when enough activity is saved. The first four
searches each have a **20% chance** of finding the chosen egg. If those miss,
the **fifth search guarantees it**. A miss still counts toward that guarantee;
pausing does not erase searches. Only the server's saved result determines
whether the egg was found.

The maximum requirement is five balanced hours. Additional time in only one
activity type cannot replace the variety requirement. The page shows your
recorded minutes and saved searches so you can choose what to do next.

## Seasonal discoveries

A seasonal discovery can be started during its displayed start window, using
your family's calendar dates. Once chosen, it has no completion deadline.
You can pause, resume and finish it after the season ends. Changes to later
offers do not change the requirements or supplies already accepted.

## After earning the egg

The completion receipt shows the actual egg, compatible potion and starter
food granted. Follow **Hatchery** to start the normal incubation and hatch
when ready. Discovery itself does not hatch a creature or shorten incubation.

If supplies cannot be delivered yet, your activity and any successful search
stay saved. Use **Try delivery again** when available. This retries the same
earned egg without another search or additional activity; viewing the page
does not retry delivery.

## Earlier rewards and supplies

Open **Earlier egg rewards** in Challenges to continue an already accepted
egg challenge or campaign. Its original terms and rewards remain saved; an
eligible saved retry also keeps its earlier promise. New system egg challenges
and later unaccepted recurring egg windows stop opening. Parent goals and
other bonuses stay separate. Today keeps the chosen discovery as the egg focus.

Eggs and potions you already own still work in the Hatchery. Paid shop requests
and already rolled expedition loot keep their saved ingredients. Unopened
Mystery Boxes keep their item effect but draw from the current non-egg pool;
an empty eligible pool leaves the box unspent.

Owning an older egg without its potion does not block discovery. You can earn
the complete package once for an unhatched appearance, even if that leaves an
extra egg. An appearance already incubating, hatched or discovered cannot start
another discovery. There is no automatic starter gift: Chick is an ordinary
choice, and rare discoveries unlock after two different species hatch.
